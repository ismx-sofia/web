In Spain, the 024 suicidal behavior helpline is available 24 hours a day. In the United States, 988. In Brazil, CVV (188).

SofIA is a journal with a social network and an artificial intelligence assistant. It is not a health service or an emergency service, and its assistant is not a person: we say so at the start of every conversation. This protocol sets out what we do when what someone writes suggests that they are in danger.

  • “I Need Help Now” is always in {path:settingsTitle}, in {path:settingsOptionHealth}, in {path:settingsOptionFamily} and below every conversation with the assistant when needed. It opens without an internet connection.
  • It shows the helplines for the country of your device and the universal ones, each with the official source that confirms it and the date on which we checked it. A wrong number on that screen is worse than none: we only add countries with an official source.
  • We never put help behind a question, a subscription or an ad.

We use a cautious check that looks for expressions of suicidal ideation or self-harm in everything that has been said in the conversation, not just in the last sentence.

  • In the artificial intelligence assistant: if it is triggered, the crisis helpline appears below the conversation, in that turn and in the following ones. The assistant does not give instructions or information that make it easier to harm oneself, and encourages asking a person for help.
  • In the wellbeing questionnaires (PHQ-8, GAD-7): the first time it is triggered, a screen asks “Are you safe right now?”, with the helplines and a visible way out. It does not lock the app, does not end the conversation and does not say which rule was triggered: a gate that punishes whoever tells the truth teaches them not to tell it.
  • We never use it for ads, for recommendations or for anything else.
  • We do not notify the guardian of a minor of any of this.
  • We do not notify anyone automatically. If a person on our moderation team, while reviewing reported content, sees that someone is in imminent danger, they may alert the emergency services, as stated in the Privacy Policy.
  • We do not diagnose or treat.
  • Help is just as close at hand.
  • A minor gets a suggested break after 60 minutes with the assistant (180 for an adult).
  • Their guardian never learns that they have sought help.

Posts, comments and messages that promote suicide, self-harm or eating disorders go against the Community Guidelines: they are removed, and when the person who posts them is talking about themselves, we offer them help before a penalty.

  • We review the helplines at least every 6 months against their official source.
  • We review the check with mental health professionals.

If you see someone in danger on SofIA, report it from the content or write to legal@sofia.ismx.app. If the danger is immediate, call 112.
