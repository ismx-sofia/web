What uses it. The AI tools you ask for: the writing aids (suggesting a title, summarising, giving you ideas, helping you keep going, rewriting and tidying up what you dictate) and the conversation with your journal. Each request uses what the model actually processes: the text it reads to answer you and the text it writes. A long request uses more than a short one; there's no fixed price per tool.

What doesn't use it. Writing and editing your entries; dictating with your phone's speech recognition, if you don't ask for it to be tidied up; what SofIA does on its own when you save an entry (transcription, the automatic summary, topics and the review under the Community Guidelines) and the writing ideas it suggests on its own; reading, commenting and everything else.
