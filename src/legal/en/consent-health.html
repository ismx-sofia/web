It is optional. It is off by default, and SofIA works just the same without it.

You give it and withdraw it with a switch: the one shown when you sign up or the one in {path:settingsTitle,settingsOptionHealth}.

That PROGRAMSAN, S.L. processes the following health data about you (Art. 9.2.a of the General Data Protection Regulation):

  • Your mood as a series over time, based on the mood you set in your entries.
  • Your wellbeing activity: minutes of reading and of mindfulness.
  • The wellbeing questionnaires you choose to take (PHQ-8 and GAD-7), with your answers and their score.
  • If you turn it on separately, the copy of your mindfulness sessions, your mood and your questionnaires in Apple Health or Health Connect. SofIA never reads data from those apps.

To show you how you are doing over time, your wellbeing activity and your questionnaire results. Nobody else sees them, they are not included in anything you share and they are never used for advertising. SofIA is not a medical device: the questionnaires help you keep a record; they are not a medical test and do not replace a professional.

Turn off the switch in {path:settingsTitle,settingsOptionHealth}. Before that, we offer you an export of your history; once you withdraw it, we stop collecting anything and delete your health data: the mood series, the wellbeing activity and the questionnaires, with their answers. We also delete what SofIA wrote to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can give it again whenever you want, and it will start from scratch.

Withdrawing it does not affect what we did with your consent before you withdrew it, nor the rest of SofIA.
