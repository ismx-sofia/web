It is optional. It is not given by default, and the rest of SofIA works just the same without it.

You give it and withdraw it from your dashboard, in {path:settingsTitle,settingsOptionFamily}.

As the mother, father or legal guardian of the minor whose account you accompany, you allow them to use Explore, the part of SofIA where people share what they write:

  • post for other people and read what they post;
  • follow and have followers, comment and send messages;
  • where the law of their country also requires your permission for it, let SofIA order what it shows them in Explore by their interests.

Always within the settings and time limits you set from your dashboard, and within what the law of their country allows, which can close Explore up to a certain age or at certain hours. You never see their messages or what they write.

From your dashboard, whenever you want. When you withdraw it, Explore closes for them, their profile stops being visible in Explore and, where the law required your permission for it, SofIA stops using their interests to order it. Withdrawing it does not affect what was done with the permission before you withdrew it.
