This guide explains how we work. It does not replace the law and creates no obligations other than those the law sets out. The Spanish version is the reference.

Write to legal@sofia.ismx.app. It is our single point of contact for the authorities of the Member States, the European Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Article 11 of the Digital Services Act) and our contact point for removal orders concerning terrorist content (Article 15 of Regulation (EU) 2021/784). While we complete the designation of an establishment (Directive (EU) 2023/1544), we also receive European Production and Preservation Orders for electronic evidence (Regulation (EU) 2023/1543) at this address. We work in Spanish and in English.

We also receive judicial notifications at our registered office.

Authenticity. Send the order from an official address of the authority: an email signed by its official domain (DKIM signature) lets us confirm where it comes from without further steps. If we cannot confirm that an order comes from the authority that signs it, we check it through an independent channel, such as a published official phone number or address, before acting on it.

  • Removal. A person on our team reviews the order and complies with it without delay. The content stops being available to other people in every country, even if the order is limited to one Member State; we say so when we report the effect, and the person concerned can ask us to review that extension.
  • Terrorist content. We comply with a removal order within one hour of receiving it (Article 3 of Regulation 2021/784). If the order arrives signed (DKIM) from the official domain of Europol or of a Spanish authority and the review has not finished after 30 minutes, the journal entries and quick thoughts it names are made private as a precaution until a person reviews it; from other domains, a person handles the order directly. We preserve the removed content and its related data for six months, or longer if the authority or a court requests it (Article 6).
  • Information. We provide only the categories of data the order asks for, within its deadline (for a European Production Order, ten days, or eight hours in an emergency), through a secure channel.
  • Preservation. We keep an encrypted copy of what the order asks for during the period the law sets: 90 days, extendable up to 180, in Spanish criminal proceedings (Article 588 octies of the Spanish Criminal Procedure Act), or 60 days, extendable by 30 at the authority's request, or as long as needed to produce the data once it confirms that the production order has been issued, for a European Preservation Order (Article 11 of Regulation 2023/1543). The account does not change because of it.
  • We inform the authority of the effect given to the order and of when it took effect (Articles 9 and 10 of the Digital Services Act; Article 3(6) of Regulation 2021/784).
  • If we cannot comply, for example because the order does not identify the content or contains a manifest error, we tell you why and ask you to clarify it.

Content removed on an authority's order does not count as a breach of our rules for the account, and its author can challenge the order before the authority or the courts of the State that issued it.

As a rule, we inform the person whose content or data the order concerns: what was requested, what we did and how they can challenge it (Articles 9 and 10 of the Digital Services Act and Article 11 of Regulation 2021/784). For European Production or Preservation Orders and for requests in Spanish criminal proceedings we do not inform the person unless the authority tells us to: the law gives that notice to the authority or imposes secrecy on us.

If the authority asks us not to inform them, because of the secrecy of a criminal investigation or for reasons of public security, we do not: the case does not appear in the person's account and they receive no notice until the authority allows it or the stated period ends. For terrorist content, that period is up to six weeks, renewable for another six (Article 11(3) of Regulation 2021/784). Please say so expressly in the order, with its reason and its duration; if the order does not say, we will ask you in writing. Every 90 days we review the cases still under secrecy and, where needed, consult the authority. Whoever reported the content still receives the outcome of their report, with no mention of the order; if that would harm the investigation, please say so.

We only provide personal data to an authority of a country outside the European Union if the request comes under an international agreement, such as a mutual legal assistance treaty, in force between that country and the Union or Spain (Article 48 of the General Data Protection Regulation), sent through the Spanish authorities. In an emergency involving an imminent danger to a person's life or physical safety we may disclose what is strictly necessary where the Regulation allows it (Article 49(1)(f)).

If a person's life or physical safety is in imminent danger, write to legal@sofia.ismx.app with URGENT in the subject, explaining the risk and what you need. We handle it as a priority.

For our part, if we become aware of information giving rise to a suspicion of a criminal offence that threatens someone's life or safety, we inform the authorities of the Member State concerned without delay or, if we cannot determine it, the Spanish authorities or Europol (Article 18 of the Digital Services Act).

If in a given year we take action against terrorist content, we publish the transparency report of Article 7 of Regulation 2021/784, with the removal orders received and what we did with them. What applies to the people who use SofIA is in the Terms and conditions and the Legal notice (in Spanish).
