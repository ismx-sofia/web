Before that, it loosens with age: each setting stops being in your hands when they reach its age (the age of digital consent, the age of majority…), and the same goes for permissions: from the age at which the minor decides them on their own, you no longer give or withdraw them; while the link lasts, those you gave remain in force until they withdraw them, and we tell them so.

When it ends, what you locked stays as their own setting, with the same value (nothing loosens by itself); the permissions you gave stop counting, including those the minor already decided on their own —whatever depended on them is closed until they give them themselves, if their age allows it—; their pending requests are canceled; and we tell them so.

When it ends because they reach the age of majority, it is different: the permissions you gave remain in force until they withdraw them themselves, and we notify them that they can do so: health in {path:settingsTitle,settingsOptionHealth} and artificial intelligence in {path:settingsTitle,settingsOptionIntelligence} (the journal is withdrawn by deleting the account).
