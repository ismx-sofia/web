FAQ

The things you ask us most

What people ask us most about SofIA, in a nutshell.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. SofIA is free and supported by ads, always clearly labeled. You decide whether they're tailored to your interests in Ajustes › Privacidad › Anuncios personalizados. What you write, your interests and your wellbeing data never travel in an ad.

Who can read what I write?

Whoever you choose, entry by entry: just you, your circle, your followers or everyone. You can post unsigned, schedule an entry for later and make your whole journal private.

Is what I write safe?

Everything travels encrypted between your phone and our servers, and your entries are read only by the people you choose. Our servers need to read what you write to transcribe it, summarize it and deliver your messages, so it isn't end-to-end encryption. We don't review your messages unless someone reports them. On your phone, you can lock your journal with your fingerprint or your face.

What does the AI do with my entries?

It transcribes your audio, summarizes and organizes your entries and helps you spot the patterns. It runs on SofIA's servers and, in part, on your own phone. Your journal is never used to train models. The AI Policy covers it in detail.

What languages is the app in?

Right now the app is in Spanish, and you can write in whatever language you like. We're getting more languages ready.

Can I take my journal with me, or delete it?

Yes, both, whenever you want. We walk you through it in Help and Delete your account.
