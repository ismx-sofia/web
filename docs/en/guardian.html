Accompanying a Minor's Account

What you declare and accept when you accompany a minor's account from your own account: what you can do, what you will never see and when it ends.

1In short#

You declare that you are the mother, the father or the legal guardian of the minor and that you exercise parental responsibility or guardianship over them.

If you link up through their invitation, the minor has to confirm that it is you. If you link up in person, with the code on their screen, the link is made immediately.

You will be able to lock their settings, give them permissions and set time limits for them, within what the law lets you decide at their age. You will never be able to read their journal, their messages or what they write.

We notify them of every change you make. And the law of their country prevails over everything: there are settings that neither you nor the minor can change.

2Who can accompany an account#

A minor's account in SofIA can be accompanied by an adult with their own SofIA account who:

  1. is of adult age in their own country and in the minor's country, verified as the law of either country requires: the date of birth you declared, the age signal from your app store or, where the law requires it, a verification with your phone's wallet;
  2. is not subject to a moderation restriction currently in force;
  3. has no block in place with the minor, in either direction;
  4. does not link up from a device that the minor uses.

A minor has, at most, a single guardian in the app at a time.

3Your declaration#

“I declare that I am the mother, the father or the legal guardian of the minor whose account I am going to accompany, that I exercise parental responsibility or guardianship over them, and that I act in their best interest.”

Making a false declaration may have legal consequences, and is grounds for closing your account. We keep your acceptance of this text, with its version and its date, as proof.

4How the link is made#

  • In person, with the code on their screen. The minor opens Ajustes › Familia and shows you a code; you scan it with your camera. The link is made immediately: being there in person is the confirmation.
  • Through their invitation. The minor shares with you an 8-character code, valid for 72 hours and for a single use. When you enter it, no link is created yet: we ask the minor to confirm that it is you.
    • The minor sees your name (or your username), and a notice telling them to tap No if they do not know you.
    • They have 72 hours to answer. In the meantime you learn nothing about their account: neither who they are nor whether they have answered.
    • If they say yes, the link is made and we notify you. If they say no, if they do not answer in time or if a condition is no longer met (for example, someone else already accompanies them), we notify you that it has not been confirmed, without telling you why.
    • If, in the meantime, another person links up in person with the code on their screen, the person who is there in person prevails and your request is closed.

5What you see and what you can do#

In your dashboard (Ajustes › Familia) you see, for each minor you accompany:

  • their name, their username and their photo, and since when you have accompanied them;
  • their privacy, content, messaging and usage time settings that the law lets you decide at their age, with their current value;
  • the permissions you have given them and that you still decide;
  • their pending requests;
  • how much time they have spent on the social features and on the artificial intelligence each day since you started accompanying them;
  • whether their account is suspended for moderation (never why or until when).

You can:

  • Lock a setting to one of the values we offer. The minor will be able to make it stricter, but not loosen it; if they want a different value, they will ask you for it. When you unlock it, the setting becomes theirs again with whatever value it has. Locking “Show Their Birthday” to off deletes the birthday from their profile (not their date of birth, which nobody sees); “Show Their Social Profiles” only hides them.
  • Give or withdraw their permissions for as long as the law lets you decide them at their age: their journal (which comes with the link and cannot be withdrawn: it is withdrawn by deleting the account), health and wellbeing, artificial intelligence and the social features.
  • Set usage time limits: minutes per day, nighttime and school hours.
  • Answer their requests: for a setting, a permission, a new contact or more time.
  • Stop accompanying them.

You never see their journal, their entries, their messages, their searches, what they read, their location, their date of birth or their exact age, or what they write in their notes to themselves.

6The law prevails#

Some settings are locked by the law of the minor's country up to a certain age (for example, adult content up to age 18). You cannot loosen those either, and the dashboard tells you so. When the minor reaches that age, the law stops locking it.

What SofIA chooses for a minor when nobody changes anything (private profile, no “Nearby”, no birthday on the profile, no autoplay…) is only the starting point: the minor can change it within what the law allows, and you can lock it.

7What the minor knows#

We notify them, in the app, of everything you do: the link, each setting you lock or unlock, each permission, each answer to their requests and the end of the link. We never share any data about you with them beyond your name, your username and your photo. They can ask our support team for help without us notifying you, and they always have crisis help at hand (Risks and how we address them).

8When it ends#

The link ends:

  • when you undo it;
  • when Apple or Google tells us that the guardian has withdrawn permission (Family Sharing or Google Play approval);
  • when you delete your account;
  • if we verify that your account belongs to a minor;
  • when the minor reaches the age of majority, within the hour following their birthday.

Before that, it loosens with age: each setting stops being in your hands when they reach its age (the age of digital consent, the age of majority…), and the same goes for permissions: from the age at which the minor decides them on their own, you no longer give or withdraw them; those you gave remain in force until they withdraw them, and we tell them so.

When it ends, what you locked stays as their own setting, with the same value (nothing loosens by itself); the permissions you gave stop counting —whatever depended on them is closed—; their pending requests are canceled; and we tell them so.

When it ends because they reach the age of majority, it is different: the permissions you gave remain in force until they withdraw them themselves, and we notify them that they can do so in Ajustes › Legal. If their account is once again recorded as belonging to a minor, those permissions stop counting and they are asked again.

If your account is subject to a moderation restriction currently in force, your dashboard is frozen: you cannot do anything, but what you gave still counts for the minor.

9Your data as a guardian#

About you as a guardian, we process:

  • that you accompany that minor, since when and how you were linked;
  • how your age was verified (the method: declared date, signal from your app store or wallet; never an identity document);
  • your acceptance of this text, with its version and its date;
  • what you do in the dashboard (settings, permissions, limits, answers);
  • while the minor has not confirmed an invitation, your account, the version you signed and the deadline (72 hours); afterwards it is closed and deleted the following day.

What for and on what legal basis: so that you can exercise parental responsibility or guardianship in SofIA and to comply with the law that requires the consent or supervision of whoever exercises it (legal obligation, Art. 6.1.c of the General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) and, where the law does not require it, on the basis of our legitimate interest in protecting the minor (Art. 6.1.f).

How long: for as long as the link lasts. When it ends, the proof of the link (that it existed, of what type and when) and your acceptance are kept, blocked, for 3 years and only for courts, public prosecutors and authorities (Art. 32 of Organic Law 3/2018, Spain's data protection law). If you delete your account, the link ends and everything else is deleted.

Your rights: those set out in the Privacy Policy, at legal@sofia.ismx.app. Data protection officer: dpo@sofia.ismx.app.

10Risks and how we address them#

  • Someone who is not their guardian. An invitation has to be confirmed by the minor, who sees who it is and can say no; the in-person code requires being there in person. The minor can write to support, without you knowing, if someone who is not their guardian is accompanying them; we review it and, where appropriate, we end the link.
  • Excessive control. You never see what they write; we notify them of every change; the law and their age gradually loosen the settings and the permissions.
  • Crisis. Crisis help is always open to the minor, and we do not tell you what they do there. How we act is set out in the Crisis Protocol.

11Changes and contact#

Each version has its number and its date, and previous versions remain published. If a change affects what you can do or your data, we will notify you in the app before it applies.
